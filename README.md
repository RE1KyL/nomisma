# Nomisma

**An Open-Source AI-Powered Research Workbench**

Nomisma is an open-source desktop research workbench designed for students and researchers in economics, business, finance, and data-intensive disciplines.

More than just an AI chatbot, Nomisma helps turn research ideas into practical workflows — from data collection and script execution to statistical analysis and visualization.

## What Makes Nomisma Different?

- **Autonomous Data Collection** — Retrieve and collect data through integrated data sources and tools.
- **Script Generation & Execution** — Generate and execute Python scripts for data processing, statistical analysis, and research tasks.
- **Integrated Research Workspace** — Work with datasets, editable spreadsheets, charts, scripts, and analytical results in one environment.
- **Specialized Analytical Tools** — Built-in capabilities for econometrics, financial analysis, quantitative modeling, and strategy backtesting.
- **General-Purpose Data Exploration** — Explore and process datasets across economics, finance, social sciences, and other fields.

## Three Research Modes

| Mode | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Economic** | Economic data, econometrics, and empirical research |
| **Finance** | Financial analysis, quantitative modeling, and backtesting |
| **Big Data** | General-purpose data processing and exploration |

# Nomisma

**一个开源的 AI 驱动型桌面研究工作台**

Nomisma 面向经济学、管理学、金融学及其他数据密集型学科的学生与研究者，致力于将 AI 能力与实际的数据研究工作流相结合。

Nomisma 不只是一个 AI 聊天工具。它能够协助用户从数据获取、脚本编写与执行，到统计分析和可视化，完成更加完整的研究任务。

## 核心特色

- **自主数据获取** —— 通过集成的数据源与工具获取、采集研究所需的数据。
- **脚本生成与执行** —— 根据研究任务生成并执行 Python 脚本，实现数据处理、统计分析与自动化计算。
- **一体化研究工作台** —— 在同一环境中管理数据集、可编辑表格、图表、脚本与分析结果。
- **专业分析工具** —— 提供计量经济学、金融分析、量化建模与策略回测等专业能力。
- **通用数据探索** —— 不局限于经济金融领域，也支持社会科学及其他领域的数据处理与探索。

## 三大工作模式

| 模式 | 主要方向 |
| --- | --- |
| **Economic** | 经济数据、计量经济学与实证研究 |
| **Finance** | 金融分析、量化建模与策略回测 |
| **Big Data** | 通用数据处理与探索 |
